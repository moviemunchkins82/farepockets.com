If your dates are flexible, the day you leave can change the price as much as the airline you pick. We looked at the cheapest one-way fare for every departure day on 28 routes departing the UK, from London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Glasgow and Belfast, and compared each day of the week.

## What we found

Across 989 daily fares for departures from October 9 to December 31, 2026:

- **Monday:** 9.1% below average, joint cheapest day to depart
- **Tuesday:** 9.1% below average, joint cheapest
- **Wednesday:** 7.8% below average
- **Thursday:** 4.1% below average
- **Sunday:** 7.2% above average
- **Saturday:** 7.3% above average
- **Friday:** 15.5% above average, the most expensive day to depart

The gap between flying out on a Monday or Tuesday and flying out on a Friday is about a quarter of a typical fare. On a £60 ticket, that is roughly £15 just for leaving on a different day.

## A real example

On [London to Glasgow](/flights/london-to-glasgow), the lowest fare we found for Tuesday, October 13 was £16. The same route on Friday, October 16 was £76. [London to Edinburgh](/flights/london-to-edinburgh) showed the same pattern that week: £16 on the Tuesday, £50 on the Friday.

## How the UK compares with the US

In [our US fare data](/guides/cheapest-day-of-the-week-to-fly), Tuesday was the cheapest day and Sunday the most expensive. From the UK, the start of the week is still cheapest, but Friday is clearly the priciest day to leave, with Saturday and Sunday next. That fits a market with lots of short trips and weekend breaks, where Friday is when many people want to go.

## How we measured it

For each route we took the cheapest cached one-way fare for each departure day, compared it with that route's average daily fare, averaged those differences by weekday, and centred the results so the seven days balance out. Comparing each route with itself keeps a £300 long-haul fare from drowning out a £15 domestic hop.

We left out departures in the first week after collection, because last-minute fares are high whatever the day, and routes with fewer than 14 days of fares (28 of our 36 UK routes qualified). The fares come from recent searches on our travel partner Aviasales, collected on October 2, 2026, and include the run-up to Christmas. Prices change constantly, so treat these numbers as a guide to patterns rather than a promise.

## How to use this

- **Leave early in the week.** Shifting a Friday departure to the Monday or Tuesday before is the simplest saving.
- **Check the calendar for your route.** Every route page, like those for [flights from London](/flights-from/london) and [from Manchester](/flights-from/manchester), shows the cheapest days over the next three months.
- **Search every London airport.** The cheapest fare is rarely from Heathrow. Here is [why searching "LON" helps](/guides/london-airports-search-lon).
