Turkey is one of the cheapest places to fly from the UK, but which airport you choose matters a lot once summer ends. We compared the fares we track to Antalya, Dalaman, Bodrum, Izmir and Istanbul on October 2, 2026, for departures from October to December.

## Antalya: cheapest, nonstop, from all four airports

Antalya was the clear winner. Every lowest fare below was nonstop:

- [London](/flights/london-to-antalya): **£19** (December 2)
- [Edinburgh](/flights/edinburgh-to-antalya): **£24** (November 14)
- [Manchester](/flights/manchester-to-antalya): **£32** (November 3)
- [Birmingham](/flights/birmingham-to-antalya): **£33** (October 30)

From London there were fares on 78 of the 91 days, and the typical (median) one-way fare fell from £65 in October to £32 in November and £44 in December.

## Dalaman and Bodrum: great in October, then almost nothing

Both airports serve the Aegean and Turquoise Coast resorts, and both had cheap October fares from London: [Dalaman](/flights/london-to-dalaman) from **£19** (October 30) and [Bodrum](/flights/london-to-bodrum) from **£32** (October 11).

After that, the fares thin out sharply. Dalaman had fares on 25 days in October, 2 in November and 1 in December. Bodrum had fares on 15 days in October, none in November and 9 in December, where the typical fare was £117. If you want winter sun on the coast, Antalya is the airport to look at.

## Izmir: a steady option for the Aegean

[Izmir](/flights/london-to-izmir) had nonstop fares from **£40** (October 31) and kept a reasonable number of dates into December. The typical fare rose from £58 in October to £68 in November and £84 in December.

## Istanbul: fares almost every day, but check which airport

[London to Istanbul](/flights/london-to-istanbul) had a fare on 90 of the 91 days from October 2 to December 31, typically £62 to £68 one-way. The lowest was **£48** with one stop, or **£53** nonstop. From the regions, [Birmingham](/flights/birmingham-to-istanbul) had a £48 nonstop fare (November 25), cheaper than the lowest nonstop from London.

Istanbul has two main airports: Istanbul Airport on the European side and Sabiha Gökçen on the Asian side. Some of the cheapest nonstop fares go to Sabiha Gökçen, including Pegasus from [Manchester](/flights/manchester-to-istanbul) (from £78) and [Edinburgh](/flights/edinburgh-to-istanbul) (from £82). Check which airport your flight uses before you book a hotel or transfer.

## Quick picks

- **Cheapest beach trip this winter:** Antalya, from any of the four UK airports.
- **Late-season coast:** Dalaman or Bodrum, but only in October.
- **City break:** Istanbul, with fares on almost any day you choose.

## How we measured it

We used the cheapest cached one-way fare for each departure day on each route, from recent searches on our travel partner Aviasales, collected on October 2, 2026. Nonstop prices come from the same searches filtered to direct flights. Prices change constantly, so treat these as a snapshot rather than a promise, and confirm the final fare before you book.
