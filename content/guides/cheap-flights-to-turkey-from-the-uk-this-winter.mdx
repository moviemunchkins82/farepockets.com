Antalya is the cheapest Turkish airport to fly to from the UK this winter, with nonstop fares from £20 from London, and Birmingham had the cheapest Istanbul flight, a £29 nonstop. Turkey is one of the cheapest places to fly from the UK, but which airport you choose matters a lot in winter. We compared the fares we track to Antalya, Dalaman, Bodrum, Izmir and Istanbul on November 4, 2026, for departures from November 2026 to January 2027.

## Antalya: cheapest, and nonstop from most UK airports

Antalya was the clear winner. All but one of the lowest fares below were nonstop:

- [London](/flights/london-to-antalya): **£20** (December 31), on easyJet from Southend
- [Edinburgh](/flights/edinburgh-to-antalya): **£28** (November 14)
- [Birmingham](/flights/birmingham-to-antalya): **£34** (November 13)
- [Manchester](/flights/manchester-to-antalya): **£44** (December 31)
- [Belfast](/flights/belfast-to-antalya): **£55** with one stop (December 31), or £60 nonstop
- [Bristol](/flights/bristol-to-antalya): **£58** (November 7)

From London there were fares on 75 of the 89 days. The typical (median) one-way fare was £54 in November, rose to £64 in December with the Christmas getaway, and fell to £42 in January, the cheapest month.

## Dalaman and Bodrum: thin until spring

Both airports serve the Aegean and Turquoise Coast resorts, and both are mostly summer airports. [Dalaman](/flights/london-to-dalaman) still had a nonstop fare from London for **£23** (December 1), but only on 15 days in November and 11 in December, with typical fares of £79 and £118, and nothing in January.

[Bodrum](/flights/london-to-bodrum) had almost nothing: fares on 9 days in November and one each in December and January, all with a stop, from **£81** (November 10). The cheapest nonstop we found was £234, for August 2027. If you want winter sun on the coast, Antalya is the airport to look at.

## Izmir: a steady option for the Aegean

[Izmir](/flights/london-to-izmir) had nonstop fares from **£48** on SunExpress from Stansted (December 31), with fares on 21 days in November, 26 in December and 7 in January. The typical fare was £77 in November, £88 in December and £63 in January.

## Istanbul: fares almost every day, and Birmingham is cheapest

[London to Istanbul](/flights/london-to-istanbul) had a fare on 86 of the 89 days, typically £67 to £75 one-way. The lowest was **£46** with one stop (December), and the cheapest nonstop was £55 on AJet, for late February.

The best Istanbul deal was outside London: [Birmingham](/flights/birmingham-to-istanbul) had a **£29** nonstop fare on easyJet (November 25). [Manchester](/flights/manchester-to-istanbul) started at £49 with one stop or £73 nonstop, and [Edinburgh](/flights/edinburgh-to-istanbul) at £61 with one stop.

Istanbul has two main airports: Istanbul Airport on the European side and Sabiha Gökçen on the Asian side. Many of the cheapest nonstop fares go to Sabiha Gökçen, including Pegasus from Manchester (£73) and [Bristol](/flights/bristol-to-istanbul) (£81), and AJet from London. Birmingham's £29 fare flies to Istanbul Airport. Check which airport your flight uses before you book a hotel or transfer.

## Quick picks

- **Cheapest beach trip this winter:** Antalya, especially in January.
- **Turquoise Coast:** Dalaman, in November or early December. Save Bodrum for spring.
- **City break:** Istanbul, with fares on almost any day, and check Birmingham before London.

## How we measured it

We used the cheapest cached one-way fare for each departure day on each route, from recent searches on our travel partner Aviasales, collected on November 4, 2026. Nonstop prices come from the same searches filtered to direct flights. Prices change constantly, so treat these as a snapshot rather than a promise, and confirm the final fare before you book.
