Georgia has three international airports that matter for a trip from the UK: Tbilisi, the capital; Kutaisi, in the west; and Batumi, on the Black Sea coast. The cheapest one depends on whether you want the lowest possible fare or the most choice of dates. We compared the fares we track on October 2, 2026.

## The lowest fares we found

From London, one-way:

- [Kutaisi](/flights/london-to-kutaisi): **£54**, departing November 24
- [Tbilisi](/flights/london-to-tbilisi): **£71** for this winter (November), and £64 for a departure in May 2027
- [Batumi](/flights/london-to-batumi): **£112**, departing November 11

From Edinburgh, the lowest fare to [Tbilisi](/flights/edinburgh-to-tbilisi) was £88 (December 14).

## Cheapest fare versus choice of dates

The single cheapest fare is not the whole story. For departures from October 2 to December 31, 2026, this is how many days had a fare and the typical (median) one-way price from London:

- **Tbilisi:** fares on 85 of the 91 days. Typical fare £140 in October, £91 in November and £115 in December.
- **Kutaisi:** fares on 26 days. Typical fare £96 in October, £70 in November and £106 in December.
- **Batumi:** fares on 20 days. Typical fare £155 in October and £144 in December, with only one November date.

So Kutaisi often wins if your dates are flexible and you can catch one of its cheap days. If your dates are fixed, Tbilisi is the safer bet, because there is a fare almost every day.

## Nonstop or one stop?

Most of the cheapest fares to Georgia have one stop. The exception in our data is Tbilisi: the cheapest nonstop fare was **£133**, on easyJet from Luton. We found no nonstop fares to Kutaisi or Batumi in recent searches, so plan for a connection there.

## Getting between them

Kutaisi is roughly a four-hour drive west of Tbilisi, and Batumi is on the coast further west again. A cheap flight into Kutaisi can still be the best deal for a Tbilisi trip, but add the bus or transfer to your budget. Flying into one airport and out of another is also worth checking if you plan to cross the country.

## Which should you pick?

- **Lowest price, flexible dates:** look at Kutaisi first.
- **Fixed dates or a nonstop flight:** Tbilisi.
- **A beach trip:** Batumi, but expect to pay more in the winter months.

You can compare all three on our [Georgia page](/destinations/georgia), which ranks the cities and UK airports by the latest fares, or check each route's calendar for the cheapest days.

## How we measured it

We used the cheapest cached one-way fare for each departure day on each route, from recent searches on our travel partner Aviasales, collected on October 2, 2026. Nonstop prices come from the same searches filtered to direct flights. Prices change constantly, so treat these as a snapshot rather than a promise, and confirm the final fare before you book.
