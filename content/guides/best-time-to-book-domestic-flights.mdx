Domestic US fares typically move most in the 1-3 months before departure. Booking too early or too late both tend to cost more than the sweet spot in the middle.

## General guidance

- **Booking window:** 1-3 months ahead for most domestic routes.
- **Cheapest days to fly:** midweek departures are usually cheaper than weekends. Our own fare data shows Tuesday and Wednesday departures running 12-17% below average; see [the cheapest day of the week to fly](/guides/cheapest-day-of-the-week-to-fly).
- **Avoid:** booking within 2 weeks of departure unless necessary. Last-minute fares are usually the most expensive.

This is general guidance, not a guarantee for any specific route. Check the lowest recent fare on a [route page](/flights/new-york-to-los-angeles) or [search your dates](/search) for current prices.
