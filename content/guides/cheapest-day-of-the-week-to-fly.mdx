If your dates are flexible, the day you leave can matter as much as the airline you pick. We looked at the cheapest one-way fare for every departure day we track on 40 popular US routes and compared each day of the week.

## What we found

Across 1,249 daily fares for departures from October to December 2026:

- **Tuesday:** 16.8% below average, the cheapest day to depart
- **Wednesday:** 12.0% below average
- **Monday:** 1.2% below average
- **Thursday:** 1.5% above average
- **Saturday:** 4.3% above average
- **Friday:** 7.4% above average
- **Sunday:** 16.2% above average, the most expensive day to depart

The gap between the cheapest and most expensive day is about a third of a typical fare. On a $150 ticket, that is roughly $50 just for leaving on a different day.

## A real example

On [New York to Miami](/flights/new-york-to-miami), the lowest fare we found for Tuesday, November 17 was $41. The same route on Sunday, November 22 was $181. Not every week is that extreme, but the pattern of cheap midweek days and expensive Sundays shows up on most routes.

## How we measured it

For each route we took the cheapest cached one-way fare for each departure day, compared it with that route's typical (median) fare, and then averaged those differences by weekday. Comparing each route with itself keeps a pricey cross-country route from drowning out a cheap short hop.

The fares come from recent searches on our travel partner Aviasales, collected on September 30, 2026. They include the Thanksgiving and December holiday weeks, which push some Sundays higher. Prices change constantly, so treat these numbers as a guide to patterns rather than a promise.

## How to use this

- **Shift your trip by a day or two.** Leaving on a Tuesday or Wednesday instead of a Friday or Sunday is the simplest saving.
- **Check the calendar for your route.** Every [route page](/flights) shows the cheapest days over the next three months, so you can see whether the midweek pattern holds for your trip. Here is [how to read it](/guides/how-to-find-the-cheapest-dates-to-fly).
- **Price each direction separately.** These are one-way fares. The cheapest day to fly home may be different from the cheapest day to leave.
