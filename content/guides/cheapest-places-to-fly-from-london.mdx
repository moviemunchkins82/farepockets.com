## Before you book

- **These are one-way fares for one specific date.** Other dates on the same route can cost far more. Check the route's calendar before you plan around a price.
- **Budget fares usually exclude bags.** Add luggage costs before comparing.
- **Most of the cheapest fares leave from Stansted, Luton, Gatwick or Southend.** Factor in getting there; our [guide to London's airports](/guides/london-airports-search-lon) explains why.
- **Prices change quickly.** These are recent lows from searches on our travel partner Aviasales, not guaranteed prices. Always confirm the final fare before you pay.
