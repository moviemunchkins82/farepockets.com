Central Asia is easier to reach from the UK than many people expect, but the cheapest fares usually mean a connection or two. We looked at the fares we track to Tashkent and Samarkand in Uzbekistan and Almaty and Astana in Kazakhstan, from London, Birmingham, Manchester and Edinburgh, on October 2, 2026.

## The lowest fares we found

One-way, cheapest UK airport first:

- [Almaty](/flights/london-to-almaty): **£101** from London (November 17). Edinburgh £114, Birmingham £127, Manchester £147.
- [Astana](/flights/london-to-astana): **£115** from London (January 14).
- [Samarkand](/flights/london-to-samarkand): **£153** from London (January 6).
- [Tashkent](/flights/birmingham-to-tashkent): **£155** from Birmingham (November 23). London £158, Manchester £177, Edinburgh £192.

Two things stand out. Birmingham edged out London for Tashkent, so it is worth checking your nearest airport rather than assuming London is cheapest. And Samarkand cost about the same as Tashkent, so you can often fly straight to the Silk Road city instead of starting in the capital.

## Nonstop flights cost two to four times as much

There are nonstop flights, but they are a different price bracket:

- **Tashkent:** Uzbekistan Airways from Heathrow and Gatwick, from **£331**, against £158 with stops.
- **Almaty:** Air Astana from Heathrow, from **£413**, against £101 with stops.

We found no nonstop fares to Samarkand or Astana in recent searches. Of the 30 cheapest London to Tashkent fares, 9 had one stop and 21 had two, so budget for a long travel day if you go for the lowest price.

## November was the cheapest month

For departures from London between October and December 2026, the typical (median) one-way fare dropped in November on every route:

- **Almaty:** £218 in October, £181 in November, £201 in December
- **Astana:** £211 in October, £162 in November, £173 in December
- **Samarkand:** £249 in October, £197 in November, £220 in December
- **Tashkent:** £229 in October, £211 in November, £226 in December

## Tips for booking

- **Compare UK airports.** The cheapest airport changed from route to route. Our [Uzbekistan](/destinations/uzbekistan) and [Kazakhstan](/destinations/kazakhstan) pages rank them by the latest fares.
- **Check the connections.** The lowest fares often combine budget airlines with one or two changes. Make sure you understand the layovers, and whether the whole trip is on one booking, before you pay.
- **Fly into one city and out of another.** Uzbekistan's cities are linked by train, so an open-jaw trip into Samarkand and out of Tashkent can work well.
- **Check entry rules.** Look at the UK government's travel advice for Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan before you book.

## How we measured it

We used the cheapest cached one-way fare for each departure day on each route, from recent searches on our travel partner Aviasales, collected on October 2, 2026. Nonstop prices and the number of stops come from the same searches. Prices change constantly, so treat these as a snapshot rather than a promise, and confirm the final fare before you book.
