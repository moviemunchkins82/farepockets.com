London has six airports, and the one with the cheapest fare is usually not the one people think of first. Searching the metro code **LON** covers all of them at once, so you see every option in one list.

## The six airports

- **Heathrow (LHR):** west London. Heathrow Express from Paddington, plus the Elizabeth line and the Piccadilly line.
- **Gatwick (LGW):** south of London. Gatwick Express from Victoria, plus Thameslink and Southern trains.
- **Stansted (STN):** north-east of London, in Essex. Stansted Express from Liverpool Street.
- **Luton (LTN):** north of London. Trains to Luton Airport Parkway, then the Luton DART shuttle to the terminal.
- **London City (LCY):** in the Docklands, closest to central London. On the DLR.
- **Southend (SEN):** in Essex. Its own railway station, with trains to Liverpool Street.

## What our fare data shows

On October 2, 2026 we pulled the 30 cheapest one-way fares for each of the 27 London routes we track, searching LON, and noted which airport each fare left from.

Where the single cheapest fare departed:

- **Stansted:** 14 routes
- **Gatwick:** 6 routes
- **Luton:** 4 routes
- **Southend:** 2 routes
- **Heathrow:** 1 route (Paris)
- **London City:** none

Across all 361 fares, 47% left from Stansted, 25% from Luton, 18% from Gatwick, 7% from Southend and just 3% from Heathrow. If you only search Heathrow, you miss almost all of the cheapest options.

## Heathrow still matters for nonstop long-haul

The picture changes if you want a direct flight. On 11 long-haul routes, the cheapest nonstop fare left from Heathrow on 8 of them. Gatwick had the other 3: Toronto, Orlando and Dubai, where the cheapest nonstop actually landed in neighbouring Sharjah.

The very cheapest long-haul fares often come with stops. To [New York](/flights/london-to-new-york), the lowest fare was £225 with one stop, against £320 nonstop from Heathrow. To [Hong Kong](/flights/london-to-hong-kong) it was £225 with one stop, against £514 nonstop. Decide whether the saving is worth the extra time before you book.

## Count the cost of getting there

A cheap fare from Stansted, Luton or Southend can look less cheap once you add the train or coach to the airport, especially for an early-morning flight. Budget airlines also charge extra for bags. Add both before comparing against a Heathrow or Gatwick fare.
